## Marathon Thief for Yamato 1.4

- Fixed the camera after Deadlock's Violet update. It froze in place or ended up inside Yamato.
- All ten looks are otherwise the same as in 1.3.

If you already have the mod, download your look again and replace the old copy.

### Pick one look
- **Thief_Default_Yamato.zip**: Default. Her standard Marathon outfit: white puffer jacket, visor and black hair.
- **Thief_endoFLEX_Yamato.zip**: endoFLEX. A synthetic look with green face plating, a charcoal jacket and blue eyes.
- **Thief_WhiteRabbit_Yamato.zip**: White Rabbit. All white, with lavender hair, red eyeliner and a white-and-pink katana.
- **Thief_KashaYokai_Yamato.zip**: Kasha Yōkai. Red armour, a full face mask and pale spiked hair. Comes with its own voice lines.
- **Thief_SlickGetter_Yamato.zip**: Slick Getter. Pale grey jacket with slate-blue legs and gloves, and teal eyes.
- **Thief_TrespassProtocol_Yamato.zip**: Trespass Protocol. Yellow puffer jacket, pale green hair and purple lips.
- **Thief_AcidAbyss_Yamato.zip**: Acid Abyss. Lime jacket over charcoal, with a white painted face and blue lips.
- **Thief_MidnightDecay_Yamato.zip**: Midnight Decay. Charcoal gear splashed with blue paint, with silver hair.
- **Thief_VoxNocturna_Yamato.zip**: Vox Nocturna. Charcoal jacket with red trim and black hair.
- **Thief_AchromaticRush_Yamato.zip**: Achromatic Rush. Black, white and grey from head to toe.

Each zip is a separate mod. Enable only one.

### Install
1. Download the zip for the look you want. Keep it zipped.
2. In Deadlock Mod Manager, click **Add Local Mod**, drop the zip in and click **Add**.
3. In **Mods Library**, click **Enable**, then start the game with **Launch modded**.

Full instructions: https://vyrena.github.io/marathon-thief-yamato/
