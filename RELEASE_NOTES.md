## Marathon Thief for Yamato 1.1

- Fixed other players' Yamatos T-posing, teleporting or crashing the game for anyone with the mod installed.
- The visor no longer lowers when crouching. It may come back in a later version.

### Install
1. Download **Thief_Default_Yamato.zip** below. Keep it zipped.
2. In Deadlock Mod Manager, click **Add Local Mod**, drop the zip in and click **Add**.
3. In **Mods Library**, click **Enable**, then start the game with **Launch modded**.

Full instructions: https://vyrena.github.io/marathon-thief-yamato/
