## Marathon Thief for Yamato 1.2

- Her eyes show their irises again. A grey layer was covering them.
- Two more looks: endoFLEX and White Rabbit, each as its own download.

### Pick one look
- **Thief_Default_Yamato.zip**: Default. Her standard Marathon outfit: white puffer jacket, visor and black hair.
- **Thief_endoFLEX_Yamato.zip**: endoFLEX. A synthetic look with green face plating, a charcoal jacket and blue eyes.
- **Thief_WhiteRabbit_Yamato.zip**: White Rabbit. All white, with lavender hair, red eyeliner and a white-and-pink katana.

Each zip is a separate mod. Enable only one.

### Install
1. Download the zip for the look you want. Keep it zipped.
2. In Deadlock Mod Manager, click **Add Local Mod**, drop the zip in and click **Add**.
3. In **Mods Library**, click **Enable**, then start the game with **Launch modded**.

Full instructions: https://vyrena.github.io/marathon-thief-yamato/
